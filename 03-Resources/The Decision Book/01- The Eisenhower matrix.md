"The most urgent decisions are rarely the most important one's" - Dwight D. Eisenhower

Here is how to use Eisenhower:
- Whatever the job lands on your desk, begin by breaking it down and then decide how ot proceed.
- we often focus too strongly on the 'urgent and Important' field on the things that have to be dealt with immediately
- ask yourself: 
	- When will i deal with the things that are important, but not urgent?
	- when will i take time to deal with important tasks before the become urgent?
