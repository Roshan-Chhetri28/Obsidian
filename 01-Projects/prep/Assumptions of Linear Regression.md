- **Linearity:** The relationship between features and the target variable is linear.
    
- **Independence:** Observations are independent of each other (no autocorrelation in residuals).
    
- **Homoscedasticity:** The variance of the residual errors is constant across all levels of the features.
    
- **Normality of Residuals:** The prediction errors follow a normal distribution.
    
- **No Multicollinearity:** Input features should not be highly correlated with one another.
